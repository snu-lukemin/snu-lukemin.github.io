Who am I?

Seonhong Min

I am a Ph.D student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering led by Prof. Yongsoo Song at Seoul National University. I graduated from Daegu Science High School in 2018, and received a bachelor's degree in Mathematics at Seoul National University. My research focuses on lattice-based cryptography, specifically fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) and its multi-party variants.
